Pricing & Rental Details

Review what your stage selection includes.

The purchase amount shown applies to the selected demo variant and its displayed price unit. Final deck, frame, support, and accessory inclusions require confirmation.

Rental subtotals use the selected variant’s rate, quantity, and number of billing periods. Deposits, GST, delivery, installation, dismantling, and collection charges are not included in that subtotal.

Adding an item to your basket does not place an order, reserve stage equipment, or confirm rental dates. Online checkout and payments are not enabled.
