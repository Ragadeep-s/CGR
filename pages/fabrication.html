Beyond the Catalogue

A conversation around your project.

Catalogue products are a starting point, but your requirement may involve a different size, connection, arrangement, finish, or component combination.

Send the available project information so the scope, material requirements, feasibility, and next steps can be discussed before a quotation is prepared.

Fabrication work is considered against the actual requirement. Acceptance, technical details, pricing, and timelines are subject to confirmation.

Discuss the Details

What does your project need?

These enquiry topics help explain your requirement. They are not a confirmation of technical capability or an accepted work order.

01

Custom Components

Share a drawing, sample reference, or measurements for a component that differs from a listed catalogue product.

02

Frames & Assemblies

Describe the intended arrangement, overall dimensions, connection requirements, and any components that must work together.

03

Project-Specific Requirements

Explain the intended application, installation environment, site restrictions, and any project documentation that needs review.

Prepare Your Enquiry

The information that helps us begin.

You do not need every detail finalized. Clearly mark anything that still needs discussion.

Project or component
A short description of what you need and its intended application.
Dimensions
Length, width, height, thickness, and relevant tolerances, with measurement units.
Drawings or references
Available sketches, drawings, photographs, sample references, or component details.
Material and finish
Required material, grade, and finish if specified, or mark these as to be discussed.
Quantity
Number of pieces, sets, or assemblies required.
Location and timeline
Delivery or project location and the preferred completion date.
Additional requirements
Installation scope, inspection documents, performance requirements, or other project conditions.

How to Begin

Share. Discuss. Confirm.

  1. 01

    Send Your Requirement

    Email your project description and available drawings or reference images. Include your contact details for follow-up.

  2. 02

    Discuss the Scope

    Clarify dimensions, materials, quantities, intended use, and any information needed before pricing can be considered.

  3. 03

    Confirm the Details

    Proceed only after the scope, specifications, quotation, responsibilities, and timeline have been agreed.

Sending an enquiry does not place an order or confirm feasibility, pricing, production, or delivery. Fabrication enquiries are handled separately from the product basket.
